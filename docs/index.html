Questions people ask

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Prayer times and the Qibla are calculated on your phone from your approximate location, so the app works on a plane, underground, or with no signal. Two small things can use the network: the town name shown above the times, which Android looks up, and the optional “type a city name” feature. The privacy policy explains both.

Which calculation method does it use?

Thirteen are built in, including Muslim World League, ISNA, Umm al-Qura, Egyptian, Karachi, Dubai, Kuwait, Qatar, Singapore, Turkey and the Moonsighting Committee. By default the method follows your school of thought (Muslim World League for Sunni, Jafari for Shia), and you can change it. The Asr rule follows your madhab.

Does it support Shia prayer times?

Yes. Jafari (Ithna Ashari) timings are built in, including the later Maghrib, and each Sunni madhab gets the correct Asr rule.

Is there a watch app?

Yes. The Wear OS app and tile calculate times on the watch itself, so they work with or without the phone nearby.

Why is it free, and what does it collect?

It is given as an ongoing charity. There are no ads, no accounts and no paid tier. The developer never receives your location, because the times are worked out on your phone. Optional usage counts are off until you switch them on, and never include your location, school of thought or settings.

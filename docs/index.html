SajdaTime

Prayer times and Qibla, calculated on your phone.
Free, offline, and with no ads or tracking.

  • The five daily prayers plus sunrise, worked out offline
  • Sunni (all four madhabs) and Shia (Jafari) conventions
  • Qibla compass corrected to true north
  • Prayer notifications, and an optional alarm mode
  • PDF timetable for today, the week, or the month
  • A Wear OS app and tile that work on their own

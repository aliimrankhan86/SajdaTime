# What to upload, and where

One folder per Play Console upload box. Everything in here is generated by
`tools/build-store-assets.sh` — **do not hand-edit these files**, edit the script or the
raw captures and re-run it.

Play Console → **Grow users → Store presence → Store listings → Default store listing**.

The English set goes in the default listing. For each other language, open **Store listings**, pick
that language (Arabic, Urdu, Turkish or Indonesian), and upload that folder's four files under
**Phone screenshots**. The folder names (`ar`, `ur`, `tr-TR`, `id-ID`) are internal; the Console asks
for the language by name. Whether a language with its own pictures shows only those is assumed, not
verified: check the listing preview after uploading.

| Folder | Play Console field | Files | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| `app-icon/` | App icon | `icon-512.png` | 512 × 512 |
| `feature-graphic/` | Feature graphic | `feature-graphic-1024.png` | 1024 × 500 |
| `phone/en-GB/` | Phone screenshots, default listing (English) | 8 files, upload all, in filename order | 1080 × 1920 |
| `phone/ar/` | Phone screenshots, Arabic listing | 4 files, in filename order | 1080 × 1920 |
| `phone/ur/` | Phone screenshots, Urdu listing | 4 files, in filename order | 1080 × 1920 |
| `phone/tr-TR/` | Phone screenshots, Turkish listing | 4 files, in filename order | 1080 × 1920 |
| `phone/id-ID/` | Phone screenshots, Indonesian listing | 4 files, in filename order | 1080 × 1920 |
| `wear-os/` | Wear OS screenshots | 4 files, in filename order | 454 × 454 |
| `tablet-7in/` | 7-inch tablet screenshots | **empty on purpose** | — |
| `tablet-10in/` | 10-inch tablet screenshots | **empty on purpose** | — |
| `chromebook/` | Chromebook screenshots | **empty on purpose** | — |

The Wear OS box only appears once the Wear form factor has been added to the app, which
happens at release time. If the tab is not there yet, upload everything else and come back.

---

## Why three folders are deliberately empty

Play marks the tablet and Chromebook boxes with an asterisk, but read the error text
underneath: *"Upload at least 2 phone or tablet screenshots."* It is **one** requirement
shared across all of them, and the phone screenshots satisfy it. The tablet and
Chromebook boxes are genuinely optional.

They are empty because **SajdaTime has no tablet layout yet**. Dropping the phone captures
into the tablet slots would show a stretched phone UI and advertise a tablet experience
that does not exist. The only cost of leaving them blank is that Play will not surface the
app in tablet and Chromebook recommendations, which is the correct outcome until there is
a real large-screen layout.

Fill these in when that layout exists, and not before.

---

## Aspect ratio is the trap

Play accepts phone screenshots at **16:9 or 9:16 and nothing in between** — that is the
Console's own wording, not an inference. A modern phone is 20:9, so a native capture off a
current device is *automatically rejected*.

This bit was wrong once. The captures came off a Pixel 7 AVD at 1080 × 2400 (2.22:1) and
were scaled down onto a 9:16 canvas, pillarboxed in brand green. That complied, but it
threw away a fifth of the frame, shrank the UI, and wrapped a green surround around an app
that never draws one.

They are now captured **natively at 1080 × 1920** and copied through untouched.

## Retaking the captures

The AVD matters, so it is scripted rather than described:

```bash
# 9:16 at 420dpi, and — the part that is easy to miss — no display cutout.
# A Pixel 7 profile forces a hole-punch whose 136px inset does not fit a 1920-tall
# screen and clips the status bar clock in half.
avdmanager create avd -n sajdastore \
  -k "system-images;android-36;google_apis;arm64-v8a" -d pixel_2 --force
```

From 1.6.0 the whole set is taken by `docs/release-tools/release-1.6.0.sh` (step 6j), straight into
`docs/store/screenshots/raw/phone/<folder>/`. What it relies on, in case you take one by hand:

- **Place is London**, written by `ScreenSeedDeviceTest` (which also completes onboarding, answers the
  usage question No and dismisses the "Does this match your mosque?" notice), not typed by hand. Picture
  07 uses `ScreenSeedDeviceTest#seed_store_alert_settings`; My prayers uses `TrackerSeedDeviceTest`.
- **Dark theme** (04 and 08): set Theme to Dark in the app's Settings, not the emulator's dark mode, and
  set it back to Follow phone afterwards.
- **Dhuhr** is ticked by tapping its row on the My prayers tab after seeding, as a tracker user would have.
- **Only on the sajdastore AVD.** Step 6j reads `wm size` and skips the whole block unless it is
  1080 × 1920, so wrong-size pictures never reach the repo.
- **Location is revoked** (`pm revoke ... ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`) after the London seed: the AVD's own GPS
  fix is Manchester and the app would move itself there. Revoked, it keeps London and Times shows no banner.
- **Status bar:** the demo bar also sets `network -e wifi show -e level 4 -e fully true`, else a wifi
  exclamation shows.
- **Picture 05 (widgets) is always by hand**, the script only prints a note and records it as MANUAL: on the
  launcher open the widget picker, tap the preview, Add; drag the Next widget's right handle to four
  columns; set the clock to 081518302026 and the demo clock to 1830; save as `raw/phone/en-GB/05-widgets.png`.
- **Clocks (London):** 15 Aug 2026 15:00 for 01, 02, 03, 06, 07; 22 Feb 2027 15:00 for 04 and 08 (07 is taken after them, back at the August clock);
  15 Aug 2026 18:30 for 05. Arabic 01 to 03: Thursday 20 Aug 2026 15:00, because Arabic weeks start on Saturday and on
  Saturday 15 Aug the My prayers week card shows only today.

The notes below are the 1.5.0 hand recipe (Manchester); the equipment is unchanged.

Then, with the app installed:

```bash
adb emu geo fix -2.2426 53.4808          # Greater Manchester
adb shell pm grant com.sajdatime.app android.permission.ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
adb shell cmd appops set com.sajdatime.app SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM allow

# Same reasoning as the exact-alarm grant, and missed on the first pass: without this,
# Settings leads with an amber "Allow sound during Do Not Disturb" banner. It is honest
# and belongs in the app; it is not what the screenshot is there to show.
adb shell cmd notification allow_dnd com.sajdatime.app

# A clean, consistent status bar. This is system chrome only — no app pixel is touched.
adb shell settings put global sysui_demo_allowed 1
adb shell am broadcast -a com.android.systemui.demo -e command enter
adb shell am broadcast -a com.android.systemui.demo -e command clock -e hhmm 1200
adb shell am broadcast -a com.android.systemui.demo -e command battery -e level 100 -e plugged false
adb shell am broadcast -a com.android.systemui.demo -e command notifications -e visible false
```

Two things that are not obvious:

- **`geo fix` expires.** The app will sit on "Finding your location…" if you send it once
  and wait. Send it repeatedly until the city name resolves.
- **Set the emulator's clock and timezone, or the times are wrong for the place.** The AVD
  inherits the host's zone, so a Manchester fix rendered in +03 puts every prayer two hours
  out — plausible-looking and false, which is the worst kind of store screenshot. And a
  capture taken at whatever time you happen to be working shows a 1 am Isha, where a
  mid-afternoon clock shows a live countdown and a Now/Next pair, which is what the screen
  actually looks like in use. Both are one command each, on a throwaway AVD:

  ```bash
  adb root
  adb shell setprop persist.sys.timezone Europe/London
  adb shell settings put global auto_time 0
  adb shell date 081515002026.00     # MMDDhhmmYYYY.ss — 15 Aug 2026, 15:00
  ```

  Re-launch the app afterwards so it picks the zone up.
- **Grant `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` before capturing**, or the Times screen carries the amber
  "the system is withholding this" banner. The banner is honest and should stay in the app;
  it just is not what the screenshot is there to show.

### Qibla screenshots

The phone capture uses the London seed at 1080 × 1920 and 420 dpi. Give the emulator a simulated magnetic
field before opening Qibla, and confirm the captured dial agrees with the turn instruction.

```bash
adb -s emulator-5554 emu sensor set acceleration 0:0:9.81
adb -s emulator-5554 emu sensor set magnetic-field -42:24:-7
```

The 12 Nov 2026 pictures show a 79° heading and 40° right turn towards the 119° London bearing. Checking
that 119 − 79 = 40 in the captured image is a free test that the London bearing is right, so do it every time.
Since 12 Nov the printed turn is worked out from the two printed numbers, so also check the dial itself: the
green hand sits 40° clockwise of the wedge and reaches the Kaaba, and the wedge stays at twelve o'clock. A short scroll in Arabic and Urdu fits the
title and Details above the floating tab bar. Copy each raw capture directly to its matching upload folder.

### Wear OS

Use the `sajdawear_large` AVD at 454 × 454 (Play wants 1:1, minimum 384). Swipe left from Times to reach Qibla. The refreshed capture
shows the live hand, Kaaba and fixed wedge. A simulated field of `39:20:-17` produced a clear gap between
the hand and wedge on the watch emulator. The bearing depends on the watch's saved location.
